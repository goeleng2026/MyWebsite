02 Concept Confirmed
FEED Design

Front-end engineering — the basis for costing, procurement and project sanction.

  • P&ID development

  • Equipment sizing & selection
  • CAPEX & Updated Feasibility
  • Functional Description

03 FEED Complete
Detailed Design

A construction-ready package — every instrument, pipe and unit specified, sized and sourced.

  • Detailed P&IDs
  • Detailed Sizing Calculations
  • Instrument & Equipment Selection
  • Safety & HAZOP Study Support
  • Suppliers, logistics & schedule
  • Functional Design Specifications (FDS)
  • Multi-Discipline Coordination
04 Build Complete
Commissioning

Pre-startup checks through to performance handover — proving the plant performs as designed under real operating conditions.

  • Pre-commissioning checks & punch lists
  • Startup & loop testing
  • Performance & acceptance testing
  • Operator training & handover
05 In Operation
Optimisation

Ongoing performance tuning once the plant is running — lifting yield, reliability and efficiency beyond the original design basis.

  • Process & control tuning
  • Yield & efficiency improvement
  • Troubleshooting & root cause analysis
  • Upgrade & expansion studies
