Three projects that illustrate the kind of work — redesign of a chemical plant after an incident, delivery of design documentation from limited source material on a coal preparation plant, and 3D FEED for an oilfield polymer hydration unit.

Chemical Industry
Chemical Industry

Waste handling plant redesign & paint cans facility optimisation

Following a 2013 incident at a chemical site, GoEdgeless Engineering led the redesign of the waste handling plant — re-working the process flow, segregation strategy, and instrumentation to bring the facility back into safe, compliant operation.

Adjacent to that work, the same site's paint cans manufacturing facility was reviewed for process optimisation: bottlenecks identified, line balancing recommended, and a redrawn set of P&IDs issued so the as-built plant matched its documentation again.

Scope 2 plants — waste + paint cans
Deliverables PFD / P&ID — Full document set
Outcome Safety-led — Brought back into compliance

Mining Industry
Mining Industry

New Wilkie Energy — coal preparation plant documentation

For the New Wilkie Energy coal preparation plant, the brief was to deliver a complete set of process engineering documents working from a limited starting reference package. The engagement included reconstructing process logic from vendor data and site walk-down, then issuing the documentation needed for procurement and construction support.

Deliverables included the full Process Flow Diagram, Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams, and Equipment / line / valve lists — drawn to the client's title block and integrated into their document control system.

Deliverable PFD — Process Flow Diagram
Deliverable P&ID — Piping & Instrumentation
Deliverable Items lists — Equipment, line & valve registers
Starting point Limited inputs — Reconstructed from vendor data

Oil & Gas
Oil & Gas

AMAST International — FEED for a polymer hydration unit

For AMAST International, GoEdgeless Engineering delivered the Front-End Engineering Design for a polymer hydration unit — the package that produces friction-reducing polymer for downhole injection in oil wells. The work spanned process definition, equipment sizing and instrumentation, through to a 3D plant layout.

The 3D layout was modelled in Autodesk Inventor to confirm equipment arrangement, operability, maintenance access and pipework routing before commitment to detailed design — a low-cost de-risking step that paid for itself in change orders avoided.

Package FEED — Front-End Engineering Design
3D Modelling Autodesk Inventor — Plant layout
Application Polymer — Friction reduction for well injection
Client AMAST International
