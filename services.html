Process engineering services
for projects that have to
be right the first time.

Three disciplines, scoped cleanly and delivered to client and industry standards. Engagements range from short scoping studies through full FEED packages and site-based design support.

01 / Service

Process Design

From conceptual studies through Front-End Engineering (FEED) and into detailed design — the engineering work that defines how a process plant operates, what equipment it needs, and how it stays safe. Suitable for greenfield, brownfield and owner-engineer engagements needing additional process capacity.

Conceptual studies & options analysisTechnology selection and preliminary feasibility reviews.

Heat & material balances, simulation supportMass and energy balances anchoring the design basis.

Equipment sizing, datasheets, line sizingFrom pumps and vessels through to hydraulic calculations.

FEED packages: SFDs, PFDs, P&IDs, instrument lists, line listsFull FEED deliverable set to support sanction and procurement.

Detailed design support through construction & commissioningOngoing engineering support as the plant is built.

HAZOP / HAZID participation and close-out trackingSupport through safety review cycles with action close-out.

02 / Service

Process Drafting

The drawings that hold the project together. Produced to client title blocks, ISA conventions and project drafting manuals — with revision discipline that fits the document control system on a big EPC project. Drafting work is available as a standalone service or bundled into a process design engagement.

Process Flow Diagrams (PFDs)Mass and energy basis captured in clean, reviewable format.

Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs)To ISA 5.1 or client-specific standards with full instrument tagging.

General Arrangement (GA) drawingsPlot plan and equipment arrangement, coordinated with other disciplines.

Loop diagrams & instrument hook-up detailsInstrument-level documentation for construction and commissioning.

Equipment lists, line lists, instrument lists, valve listsRegister-format deliverables integrated with the drawing set.

Revision management and IFR / IFA / IFC issue cyclesControlled issue cycles with comment registers and close-out.

03 / Service

Process Solutions

Engineering interventions on existing assets. Where Process Design defines a new plant, Process Solutions improves one you already have — safely, with operations in the room, and with constructability accounted for. Often the right starting point when a site is hitting capacity, has had an incident, or has accumulated informal changes that the P&IDs no longer reflect.

Plant debottlenecking & capacity uplift studiesIdentify and resolve throughput constraints using operating data.

Site redesign following incidents or safety findingsRedesign work with safety outcomes as the primary driver.

P&ID redlining, as-built reconciliation & document refreshBring documentation back in line with what's actually installed.

Process optimisation — yield, energy, throughputSystematic review to extract performance from existing equipment.

Modification packages for safe execution during shutdownsEngineered change packages with isolation, sequence and commissioning plans.

Engineering support to operations & reliability teamsEmbedded or retainer support for ongoing operational challenges.

Our Approach

A predictable engineering process, on every job.

No surprises. The same four-step rhythm, scaled to the size of the package.

01

Scope

Call to confirm deliverables, schedule, standards and interfaces. Fixed-scope estimate issued within one business day.

02

Design

Engineering executed against agreed standards. Calculations, models and drafts produced in your format, with clean revision control.

03

Review

Internal review before issue. Participation in client design reviews, HAZOP / HAZID workshops and IDC comment cycles.

04

Issue

Deliverables against agreed milestones — IFR, IFA, IFC — with full revision comment register and a clean hand-back.
